Privacy Policy Welcome to Lightning Renamer. We take your privacy very seriously. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.
1. Data Collection and Processing Lightning Renamer strictly abides by the Privacy-by-Design philosophy. We do not collect, process, or store any personal data. Specifically:
    100% Local Processing: All file reading, renaming, and writing operations run securely constrained within your local device. Under no circumstances are files or filenames uploaded to cloud servers. Zero Telemetry: We do not log or collect device fingerprints, OS information, crash reports, or usage analytics.
2. Data Sharing and Disclosure Because the application structurally avoids collecting any user information, it is both logically and legally impossible for us to sell, trade, or otherwise disclose data to third-party networks, advertisers, analytics entities, or law enforcement. 3. Data Security Lightning Renamer operates under macOS Sandbox restrictions. The app can only interact with directories you explicitly grant access to, firmly mitigating risks of unauthorized filesystem scanning or tampering. Given the offline nature of our architecture, the risk of a server-side data breach on our end is fundamentally non-existent. 4. Policy Updates We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our application or relevant legal frameworks. When material revisions are made, the updated version will be posted on this page along with the effective "Last Updated" date.
